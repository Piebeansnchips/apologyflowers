A little about us

Born out of necessity.

Apology Flowers was founded following years of personal experience, repeated misunderstandings and an frankly unsustainable expenditure on flowers.

Our founder, Jim, understands that life doesn't always go to plan. Words are sometimes insufficient, gestures are occasionally required, and some situations simply call for a really rather impressive bunch of flowers.

Our philosophy is simple: take responsibility, make amends, and never underestimate the power of a well-presented bouquet.

Apology Flowers. Making things better, one bouquet at a time.
